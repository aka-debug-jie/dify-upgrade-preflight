# P05 decision readiness

## Decision: FORMAL_SCOPE_CHANGE

The current two exact candidate edges are technically useful enough to continue evidence work, but they are not enough to support the stated v0.1 value threshold. They cover two candidate rule directions, while the six historical value scenarios include four additional or only-generic/manual areas. The correct next decision is a formal, bounded scope change/re-charter, not `GO_CURRENT_SCOPE`, expansion without a charter, or project `NO_GO`.

## Answers

1. **Are the two exact edges enough for a valuable v0.1?** Not yet. They may be retained as a narrow pilot after adjudication, but the current package has no approved rule, edge, or independently adjudicated case and cannot demonstrate the six-scenario value threshold.
2. **Do all six P05 root families have sufficient evidence?** No. R01/R02 taxonomy requires owner confirmation; R02, R03 and R04 have fact-semantic mismatches; R04–R06 depend on attestations; all source applicability/severity decisions remain unapproved.
3. **Historical capability counts:** generic detection=2; rule candidates=2; manual-only=1; uncovered=1. None is an approved product rule.
4. **Why `FORMAL_SCOPE_CHANGE`?** It preserves the Conditional GO product thesis while honestly resetting P05 around a selected maximum of two exact paths and a measurable value set. `GO_CURRENT_SCOPE` would overstate evidence; `EXPAND_CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE` without a charter risks unbounded scope; `NO_GO` is not warranted because the architecture and two candidate directions remain viable.

## Minimum owner decisions

1. Choose whether the next bounded P05 charter retains, revises or retires P05-EDGE-A and P05-EDGE-B; select no more than two exact paths, including any historical scenarios newly brought into scope.
2. Decide whether P05-R01 and P05-R02 are independent root-cause families, and reject or require revision of R02/R03/R04 fact semantics before case adjudication.
3. For each retained root, adjudicate the proposed expected result/severity/phase and select a heldout subset before implementation. The pending copy contains all 33 decisions as null placeholders.
4. Accept or reject the renewed source transport records as candidate evidence only, then separately approve exact source-to-rule applicability before any catalog promotion.

No rule, support edge, case outcome, phase completion, or P06 work is authorized by this decision document.
